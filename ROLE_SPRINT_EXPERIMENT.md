# RoleSprint 30-Day Validation Experiment

Status: implementation-ready product experiment; no outcome is claimed before data
is collected.

## Product hypothesis

Technical learners are more likely to return and complete deliberate practice when a
course library is replaced by a role-specific daily loop:

1. understand a concept;
2. build something;
3. use an AI assistant as a critic or collaborator;
4. independently verify the result; and
5. rehearse an interview explanation.

## First cohort

- One primary audience: engineers actively preparing for a role change or interview.
- Target pilot cohort: 15 accepted learners; publish the denominator even if
  fewer complete the study.
- Duration: 30 days.
- Minimum commitment: 30 focused minutes per day.
- No paid testimonial, fabricated account, or founder/test account in metrics.

## Metric definitions

| Metric | Exact definition |
|---|---|
| Accepted learner | A real person who explicitly agrees to participate |
| Activated learner | Creates a plan and completes at least one full daily mission |
| Day-7 retained | Completes any mission step on days 6–8 after activation |
| Day-14 retained | Completes any mission step on days 13–15 after activation |
| Day-28 retained | Completes any mission step on days 27–30 after activation |
| Mission completion | All five steps for one day are checked |
| Practice minutes | Sum of the fixed planned minutes for completed steps; not a claim of observed clock time |
| Interview outcome | Learner-reported interview or offer, stored separately and labeled self-reported |
| Paid conversion | A completed payment, not stated willingness to pay |

## Privacy and integrity

- The product stores plan state in the learner's browser.
- Analytics is opt-in and sends only allow-listed event fields.
- Code, answers, notes, email, exported logs, and free text are never sent as analytics.
- Shared links contain only role, duration, and planned daily minutes.
- Exported logs state that they are learner-owned practice records, not certifications.
- Raw exports, definitions, date ranges, exclusions, and screenshots must be preserved
  before any metric is used in an investor or immigration document.

## Decision rule

Continue investing in RoleSprint only if the cohort produces both:

- at least 10/15 activated learners, 6/15 Day-7 retained learners, and 4/15
  Day-14 retained learners (reported as counts as well as percentages); and
- qualitative evidence that the Learn → Build → AI → Verify → Interview loop solves a
  problem learners cannot solve as easily with a generic course library or chatbot.

If retention is weak, interview every churned participant and change the product
before claiming traction.

These are product-learning thresholds, not statistical proof of efficacy and
not evidence of field-level significance. A later outcome study needs a
predefined skills assessment, a larger cohort, and independent review.
