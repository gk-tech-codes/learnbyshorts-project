# LearnByShorts: O-1 Evidence Development Plan

This is a product evidence plan, not legal advice. An experienced U.S.
immigration attorney should determine whether any record satisfies an O-1
criterion and how the totality of the evidence should be presented.

Official references:

- USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 2, Part M, Chapter 4:
  https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-2-part-m-chapter-4
- USCIS O-1 overview:
  https://www.uscis.gov/forms/explore-my-options/o-1-visa-individuals-with-extraordinary-ability-or-achievement

## What the product can honestly support

LearnByShorts may become useful supporting evidence, but owning or launching a
website does not by itself establish extraordinary ability. The strongest
record will combine documented product work with independent proof that the
work is important beyond the founder's own statements.

### Original contributions of major significance

Potential product evidence:

- A clearly documented learning method and technically original implementation.
- A documented AI Fluency method that produces inspectable learner artifacts
  and measures verification behavior rather than passive content consumption.
- Independent adoption by schools, educators, employers, or developer groups.
- Reproducible learner outcomes, with a defined methodology and honest limits.
- Expert letters that explain specifically what is original, how the expert
  knows, and why the contribution matters to the field.
- Citations, integrations, forks, adaptations, or curricula that rely on the
  work.

Weak evidence to avoid treating as sufficient:

- A self-authored feature list.
- Website traffic without evidence of field-level significance.
- Generic recommendation letters or testimonials.

### Authorship of scholarly articles

Possible path:

- Publish a rigorous article describing the learning design, implementation,
  evaluation method, limitations, and results.
- Prefer a reputable peer-reviewed venue or established professional
  publication where appropriate.
- Archive the published version, editorial record, citations, and author page.

A product blog post can document the work, but should not automatically be
described as a scholarly article.

### Critical or essential role for a distinguished organization

Possible path:

- Document a real role and specific responsibilities within an organization
  that has an independently established distinguished reputation.
- Preserve evidence that the role and contributions were critical, not merely
  the title "founder."
- Use organizational records and detailed independent letters rather than a
  self-created title alone.

### Published material about the beneficiary

Possible path:

- Seek legitimate editorial coverage focused on the person and the work.
- Preserve the publication's audience, date, author, URL, and full article.
- Keep independent coverage separate from sponsored or self-published content.

### Judging the work of others

Possible path:

- Serve as an invited hackathon judge, technical reviewer, program committee
  member, or evaluator in a closely related field.
- Preserve the invitation, judging criteria, completed work, organizer details,
  and event reputation.

## Twelve-month evidence program

1. Establish the baseline in `docs/IMPACT_LEDGER.md`.
2. Publish releases with dates, test evidence, architecture decisions, and
   security posture.
   Preserve `docs/AI_FLUENCY_CURRICULUM.md`, the interactive Skill Studio,
   screenshots, source attribution, and the corresponding Git history as
   technical authorship evidence—not as proof of significance by themselves.
   Preserve the RoleSprint algorithm, validation protocol, privacy design,
   automated tests, dated screenshots, and Git history on the same basis.
3. Run small, permissioned pilots with educators or developer communities.
4. Measure activation, completion, return behavior, and learning outcomes
   consistently; report failures and limitations as well as successes.
5. Publish a substantive technical or educational-method article.
6. Invite independent experts to evaluate the product after they have actually
   reviewed or used it.
7. Pursue legitimate talks, judging invitations, awards, and editorial
   coverage—never pay for or manufacture evidence.
8. Review the growing record with immigration counsel before characterizing it
   as satisfying any criterion.

## Monthly review questions

- What changed in the product, and where is the dated technical record?
- What measurable user outcome improved?
- Which evidence is independently verifiable?
- Did any outside organization adopt, cite, evaluate, or publish the work?
- Are metric definitions unchanged and raw exports preserved?
- Is any statement stronger than the underlying evidence? If yes, correct it.
