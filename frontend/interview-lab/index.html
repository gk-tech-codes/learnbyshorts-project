What teams evaluate

Prepare for the whole job.

A strong candidate does more than reach an answer. They make their reasoning observable, test assumptions, and respond well when the problem changes.

01

Live coding

Clarify the problem, choose a data structure, implement cleanly, test aloud, and improve complexity.

Algorithms · data structures · testing
02

Debugging & review

Read unfamiliar code, form hypotheses, isolate the defect, explain risk, and propose a maintainable fix.

Diagnosis · code quality · collaboration
03

System design

Move from requirements to interfaces, data models, scale, failure modes, observability, and tradeoffs.

Architecture · reliability · judgment
04

Behavioral impact

Tell evidence-rich stories about ownership, conflict, decisions, failures, learning, and measurable results.

Leadership · communication · reflection
05

Project deep-dive

Defend what you built: constraints, alternatives, your contribution, the hardest failure, and what changed afterward.

Depth · credibility · product thinking
06

AI-assisted judgment

When tools are allowed, show that you can verify output, protect data, catch subtle errors, and remain accountable.

Verification · security · responsible use
