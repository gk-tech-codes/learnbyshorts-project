Questions

Know what the planner does—and doesn’t do.

Does the job description leave my browser?

No. RoleSprint detects a small set of technical skill phrases locally. When you create the plan, the original text is cleared; it is never saved in progress data or analytics.

Is the starting-fit score a hiring prediction?

No. It is a planning estimate based on your own ratings and the selected role. It helps order practice; it does not predict an interview or hiring outcome.

Does this replace a full subject course?

No. RoleSprint is a complete practice schedule for a target role, not a claim that a few cards teach an entire technical field. It shows what to practice, what to produce, and how to verify it from start to finish.

Can I use RoleSprint without creating an account?

Yes. The planner is free and anonymous. Your browser stores the generated roadmap and checked steps so you can return on the same device.
