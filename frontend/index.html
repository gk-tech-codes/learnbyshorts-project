Yes, on this browser and device. Learning OS can turn that progress into the next mission. Signed-in learners can sync eligible structured progress without uploading free-form drafts.

No. Its adaptive mission and mastery calculations run in your browser. It can generate a tutor prompt to use with the AI assistant you already choose.
