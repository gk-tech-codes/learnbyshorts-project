## Intended outcome

A company-aware interview plan, two recorded simulations, and an evidence-backed project narrative.

## Why this path is current

Hiring loops increasingly measure real engineering judgment beyond isolated algorithm recall.

## Proof missions

### 1. Diagnose the weakest signal

- Action: Score coding, debugging, systems, project depth, behavior, and AI judgment honestly.
- Evidence to produce: A prioritized two-week plan.

### 2. Design and debug

- Action: Design one service, then diagnose a realistic failure inside it.
- Evidence to produce: An architecture plus hypothesis-driven incident review.

### 3. Defend a real project

- Action: Explain constraints, your contribution, alternatives, failure, and measured impact.
- Evidence to produce: A ten-minute recorded deep-dive.

### 4. Run the full simulation

- Action: Combine a timed technical task with behavioral follow-up and self-review.
- Evidence to produce: A rubric score and targeted repeat.

### Debugging Like A Pro

- Creator: ByteByteGo
