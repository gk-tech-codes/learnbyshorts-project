Four-mission proof sprint

Do the work. Keep the evidence.

Check a mission only after the proof exists. Progress stays in this browser and also appears in the Modern Skills catalog.

  1. 01

    Write the implementation contract

    Turn a feature request into scope, invariants, interfaces, acceptance checks, and explicit non-goals before prompting.

    Proof: A task brief another engineer can execute without guessing.
  2. 02

    Constrain the generated change

    Ask for one vertical slice, preserve unrelated code, and require the agent to explain every touched file.

    Proof: A focused diff with a file-by-file rationale.
  3. 03

    Test what the model may miss

    Inspect the diff for boundary, security, concurrency, and failure-state risks, then add targeted tests.

    Proof: At least three risk-derived tests including one caught defect.
  4. 04

    Own the final review

    Reproduce the behavior, remove unnecessary complexity, record rejected suggestions, and explain the final design unaided.

    Proof: A review summary and independent five-minute walkthrough.
