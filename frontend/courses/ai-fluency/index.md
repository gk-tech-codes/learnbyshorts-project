## Intended outcome

A reusable AI workflow with a verification checklist and documented failure boundaries.

## Why this path is current

AI use is becoming baseline; dependable judgment is the differentiator.

## Proof missions

### 1. Map capability and failure

- Action: Test one task across two assistants and record where each succeeds, guesses, or refuses.
- Evidence to produce: A comparison matrix with three verified failure modes.

### 2. Engineer the context

- Action: Turn a vague request into a goal, constraints, evidence, output contract, and stopping rule.
- Evidence to produce: A reusable prompt template plus one before-and-after result.

### 3. Build a verification gate

- Action: Check sources, edge cases, security, and reproducibility before accepting an output.
- Evidence to produce: A completed five-gate review.

### 4. Make the final decision

- Action: Write what the model contributed, what you rejected, and why the result is safe to use.
- Evidence to produce: A short decision record with explicit human ownership.
