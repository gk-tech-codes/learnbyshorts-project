Four-mission proof sprint

Do the work. Keep the evidence.

Check a mission only after the proof exists. Progress stays in this browser and also appears in the Modern Skills catalog.

  1. 01

    Map capability and failure

    Test one task across two assistants and record where each succeeds, guesses, or refuses.

    Proof: A comparison matrix with three verified failure modes.
  2. 02

    Engineer the context

    Turn a vague request into a goal, constraints, evidence, output contract, and stopping rule.

    Proof: A reusable prompt template plus one before-and-after result.
  3. 03

    Build a verification gate

    Check sources, edge cases, security, and reproducibility before accepting an output.

    Proof: A completed five-gate review.
  4. 04

    Make the final decision

    Write what the model contributed, what you rejected, and why the result is safe to use.

    Proof: A short decision record with explicit human ownership.
