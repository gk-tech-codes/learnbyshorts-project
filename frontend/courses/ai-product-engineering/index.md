## Intended outcome

A deployed thin-slice AI product whose quality and cost can be measured.

## Why this path is current

Successful AI products need product judgment, evaluation, and operations—not only model access.

## Proof missions

### 1. Define the user decision

- Action: Choose one narrow user problem and specify what a useful, harmful, and abstaining answer looks like.
- Evidence to produce: A product brief with an initial evaluation set.

### 2. Ground the response

- Action: Add retrieval only where current or private source material is actually required.
- Evidence to produce: A citation-aware prototype and retrieval failure cases.

### 3. Measure quality and cost

- Action: Track latency, cost, task success, unsupported claims, and user correction rate.
- Evidence to produce: A small scorecard with thresholds.

### 4. Ship the thin slice

- Action: Deploy with access control, logging boundaries, fallback behavior, and a kill switch.
- Evidence to produce: A live demo plus an operational readiness review.
