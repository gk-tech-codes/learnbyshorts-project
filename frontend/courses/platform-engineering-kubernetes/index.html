Four-mission proof sprint

Do the work. Keep the evidence.

Check a mission only after the proof exists. Progress stays in this browser and also appears in the Modern Skills catalog.

  1. 01

    Start with developer friction

    Interview or simulate one service team and rank its recurring setup, deploy, security, and operations pain.

    Proof: A friction map with one measurable platform objective.
  2. 02

    Run the workload safely

    Package one service and define requests, limits, health probes, configuration, identity, and rollout behavior.

    Proof: A reviewed Kubernetes workload with a failed-health recovery test.
  3. 03

    Create one golden path

    Turn the secure defaults into a reusable service template with documented escape hatches.

    Proof: A self-service template that produces a deployable service.
  4. 04

    Measure platform value

    Track lead time, deployment reliability, adoption, support burden, and one developer-experience signal.

    Proof: A platform scorecard and a decision to improve, keep, or remove one feature.
