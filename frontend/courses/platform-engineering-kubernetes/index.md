## Intended outcome

A small internal platform blueprint with one golden path, self-service contract, policy gates, and measurable developer feedback.

## Why this path is current

Kubernetes is a core infrastructure layer for cloud and AI workloads, while platform value depends on reducing developer friction rather than adding another portal.

## Proof missions

### 1. Start with developer friction

- Action: Interview or simulate one service team and rank its recurring setup, deploy, security, and operations pain.
- Evidence to produce: A friction map with one measurable platform objective.

### 2. Run the workload safely

- Action: Package one service and define requests, limits, health probes, configuration, identity, and rollout behavior.
- Evidence to produce: A reviewed Kubernetes workload with a failed-health recovery test.

### 3. Create one golden path

- Action: Turn the secure defaults into a reusable service template with documented escape hatches.
- Evidence to produce: A self-service template that produces a deployable service.

### 4. Measure platform value

- Action: Track lead time, deployment reliability, adoption, support burden, and one developer-experience signal.
- Evidence to produce: A platform scorecard and a decision to improve, keep, or remove one feature.
